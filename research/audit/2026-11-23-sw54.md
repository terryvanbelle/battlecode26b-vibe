## Active bugs (code and doc disagree, and the code decides sw54's spawns)

1. **The core count is cut by the cap.**
   - *What the docs say:* Cfg says CORE_BABIES are "kept unconditionally (while CORE_FLOOR cheese remain) before the
     income test applies".
   - *What the code does:* King.spawn tests babyCap after the boot. With no enemy in the king's view, the cap is
     MAX_BABIES 16 before round 300 (the rich tier is closed), and after it while the bank is under RICH_BANK. So
     sw54's core of 20 keeps 16.
   - *Already in sw53:* its core of 18 met the same cap of 16.
   - *Fix newcomer:* yl15 (CORE_OVER_CAP): the core boot is not cut by the cap.
2. **MAX_BABIES_WAR is a siege cap.**
   - *What the docs say:* Cfg reads "Baby cap in peace and at war", and babyCap's doc reads "the war or peace cap".
   - *What the code does:* King.spawn passes its own `war` to babyCap: at war with an enemy in the king's view, and
     the war floor paid.
   - *Effect:* sw54's raise to 20 holds only in siege. The rest of the war keeps MAX_BABIES 16.
   - *Why nobody saw it:* sw53 had both caps at 16, so the difference never showed.
   - *Fix newcomer:* yl16 (WAR_CAP_ALL): MAX_BABIES_WAR caps the whole war (G.coop false).

The tournament decides which reading wins. If neither fix rates above sw54, the next newcomer on sw54 corrects the two
docs instead. That change is behaviour-neutral.

## Latent special values and steps

**Steps of 4.** The engine raises the rat cost 10 for every 4 baby rats. The king's estimate of its babies is
therefore 4 × floor(n / 4), less one for each king past the first. A core count or cap v keeps babies while the
estimate is below v. With k kings, it acts as the multiple of 4 at or above v + k − 1.

| switch | sw54 | special values and steps |
|---|---|---|
| CORE_BABIES | 20 | **sw54's change from 18 does nothing with one king:** every value from 17 to 20 keeps 20 babies. With two or three kings, 20 keeps 24 where 18 kept 20. Even then it acts only where the cap allows (bug 1): in siege, or with the rich tier open. |
| MAX_BABIES_WAR | 20 | It is a siege cap (bug 2). It keeps 20 babies with one king and 24 with two. A value at or below MAX_BABIES makes siege stricter than the rest of the war. 0 = no spawn at all in siege before the rich tier opens (undocumented, unused). |
| RICH_BANK | 600 | **Compared with the whole bank, while every floor counts per king.** With two kings, the spawn floor (300 × 2) already reaches 600. So from round 300, every spawn that clears the floor gets the rich cap (40). In sw53 the floor was 400 × 2 = 800, under 1000, so the rich gate still held with two kings. 0 = the rich cap from RICH_FROM, whatever the bank. |
| KING_RESERVE | 300 | **No longer binds any spawn.** It is the least spawn floor (`spawnFloor = max(f, KING_RESERVE)`), and sw54's 300 equals CORE_FLOOR, WAR_FLOOR and SPAWN_FLOOR. sw53's 400 lifted all three to 400, so the change lowers every spawn floor by 100 per king. Its other use is the babies' trap gates: a rat trap from our half needs reserve + 20, and a cat trap needs reserve + 10 (or CAT_LAY_FLOOR). 0 = off, as documented. |

  more, and a king whose army holds is less often cut off. Kept.
- **More cat traps.** The lower trap gate (reserve + 10) gives 40% more cat traps per game. Rat traps are unchanged:
  TRAP_CAP and the forward traps decide those. Kept.
