# Bug audit of sw53 (new champion, owner 11-23 new-champion clause)

sw53 was crowned at 09:14 PDT: +32 vs yk1 on 383 games, against the > +21 it needed. It was submitted to the replica
as #453 (accepted). This is the first audit under the owner's 11-23 rule: every new champion gets a bug audit, a
test-compliance check and a test-coverage check, and fixes enter the tournament as newcomers.

**What sw53 is:** yk1 + `RAID_SHARE 2` + `FWD_TRAP`.
- *RAID_SHARE 2 (raiders):* at war, half the gatherers and fighters take the enemy twin of their home mine.
- *FWD_TRAP (forward traps):* a rat trap laid from the enemy half skips the KING_RESERVE and austerity gates.
- *Origin:* built at 07:15 as a sparring partner imitating the replica teams that beat us
  (research/field/sparring-gap).
- *Size:* 22 lines differ from yk1 (Baby.java 15, Cfg.java 7). Everything else is yk1's code, audited this morning
  in [2026-11-23-yk1.md](2026-11-23-yk1.md).

## Mirror-symmetry check

- **Raider draw:** it uses slot bits 12-13. The slot comes from the mirror-invariant birth seed (the round plus the
  unsigned offset from the king), so twin rats on the two sides draw alike. OK.
- **The twin and the home half agree:** both use `firstSym()`, so "home half" and "twin" always refer to the same
  symmetry. Before the map's symmetry is resolved, both follow the first live candidate (ROT, then HOR, then VER).
  RaidTest pins this: with ROT and HOR both live, the twin is the ROT mirror.
- **Latent, both sides alike, not measured:** on a map whose true symmetry is HOR or VER, a raider that picks
  before ROT is ruled out walks to the ROT twin. It moves on only when the mine proves idle, the walk stalls, or
  the mine list changes.

## Latent special values

| switch | sw53 | special values |
|---|---|---|
| RAID_SHARE | 2 | 0 = off (yk1's play). 1-3 = that many quarters of the gatherers and fighters at war. **4 or more = all of them**: the `(slot >>> 12) & 3` draw is always below 4. This is undocumented, and sw55 plays 4. Negative = off. |
| FWD_TRAP | true | false = yk1's gate on both halves. |

## Misleading or dead code (no behaviour change)

1. **The war-start reset repeats raider()'s slot test inline.** The reset in mineMove (Baby.java:1221) leaves out
   raider()'s role test. That is neutral: mineMove runs only for gatherers and fighters (Baby.java:1131), and a
   failed rusher becomes a gatherer before it gets there. Cleanup: call `raider()`.
2. **Cfg still labels both switches as sparring.** They read "SPARRING sw53" / "SPARRING sw51", but they are the
   champion's switches now.
3. **pickMine's comment overstates "contested".** It says "a raider holds the enemy twin of its contested home
   mine".
   - *Gatherer raiders (most raiders):* they take the twin of whatever home mine their slot picks, or keep an
     enemy-half pick as is.
   - *Fighter raiders:* the contested filter holds only until the first enemy-half mine is known (yk1 bug 1).

Items 1 and 2 change no play. They ride along in yl12 (below), so the champion stays identical to what was
submitted (#453). Item 3 is also a choice of play, and yl12 tests it.

## Checked and kept

- **FWD_TRAP skips the reserve and austerity gates.** This is by design: the teams that beat us lay 54-76% of their
  traps in our half. The RAT_TRAP_MIN_CHEESE floor (`Econ.floor(100)`) and TRAP_CAP still apply.
  - *Starvation:* sw53's king took starvation damage in 50 of 369 games (14%; yk1 10%), and sw53 won 1 of those
    50. yl7 (FWD_TRAP alone on yk1) never starved in 16 games; yl11 (the raiders alone) starved in 2 of 20.
  - *Reading:* the extra starvation follows the raiders' lower income, not the forward traps. Starving marks a
    game already being lost. Kept.
- **The raiders' price.** Per game against yk1's line:

  | per game | sw53 | yk1 | change |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | cheese picked up | 11.4k | 13.7k | −17% |
  | cheese handed to the king | 8.6k | 10.7k | −19% |
  | rats spawned | 149 | 163 | −8% |
  | damage dealt to enemy kings | 267 | 245 | |
  | damage taken by our king | 212 | 221 | |
  | games won | 56% | 54% | |

  This is the trade sw53 was built for: the top profile's H1 says holding the enemy half decides games. Kept.
- **Rushers never raid.** raider() needs a gatherer or a fighter. A rusher that gives up (RUSH_LIFE) becomes a
  gatherer and raids by its slot like any other.
- **Centre-line mines.** homeHalf counts ties as home, so a mine on the centre line is "home" for both sides. Its
  twin is on the line too, so the raider stays there. Harmless.

## Tests (11-23, at the crowning)

- **Compliance.** `bash tools/unit-tests.sh` with ratbot = sw53 first failed one check: MechTest "fight post:
  gatherers spread over every mine".
  - *Cause:* the test's slots 0-5 all draw raider bits under RAID_SHARE 2, so its gatherers took enemy twins. That
    is sw53's behaviour, changed on purpose.
  - *Changed:* the test's rats now carry slot bits 3 (no raider), which keeps the same picks (12288 is a multiple of
    2 and 3). The gatherer check is skipped when RAID_SHARE is 4 or more, since then no slot is free of raiding.
- **Coverage:**
  - **RaidTest** (new, 31 checks):
    - `raider()` over the 4 slot draws × gatherer/fighter/attacker × war/coop;
    - pickMine: a raider's home mine becomes its twin, slot bits 3 keep it, coop keeps it, and an enemy-half pick
      stays;
    - the twin under HOR alone, and under ROT when ROT and HOR are both live;
    - a fighter raider holds the twin of a contested home mine.
  - **FwdTrapTest** (4 checks, moved from test/yl7): below the reserve, and when austere, no trap from the home
    half and a trap from the enemy half.
  - *Reflection:* both tests read their switch by reflection, so bots without it (the yk1 line: yk1, yl1, yl5,
    sw50, sw52) still compile the suite. RaidTest reports "skipped" there, and FwdTrapTest expects the gate to hold
    on both halves.
- **Runner fix.** tools/unit-tests.sh now skips an empty test/BOT folder. After the move, test/yl7 was empty, and
  the unexpanded glob went to javac as a file name.
- **Results:**
  - `bash tools/unit-tests.sh` (ratbot = sw53) passes 2010 bot checks: BotTest 1404, CheeseTest 4, FixTest 512,
    FwdTrapTest 4, MechTest 55, RaidTest 31. It also passes all 864 tool checks.
  - The pool bots pass too: `unit-tests.sh yl1`, `yl5` (2038 checks, with its own CheeseTest 7 and AuditTest 56), `yl7`,
    `yl12` (with its RaidPostTest 2) and `sw55` (MechTest 54: the skipped gatherer check).
- **Not covered by a test:**
  - the war-start reset in mineMove (private; it runs the whole mine walk);
  - the raider's walk to and from the enemy half, and its delivery;
  - the wrong-twin case while the symmetry is unresolved (only the firstSym order is pinned).
