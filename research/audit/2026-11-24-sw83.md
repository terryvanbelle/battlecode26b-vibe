**Bugs found: one (the look's window), fixed in the newcomer sw85.**
1. *Logic:*
   - **The look counts rounds, not turns.** look() runs while `G.round - pickRound < SENTRY_LOOK`, and pickRound is
     the round of the pickup. A pickup ahead of the move (the turn-start pickUp, Baby.java:79) is followed by that
     round's move decision, so the rat looks on rounds r, r+1 and r+2: three turns, the three quadrants it has not
     seen. A pickup after the move (actAfterMove's pickUp, Baby.java:213, when the rat has walked up to the pile)
     comes after that round's move decision, so the rat looks on rounds r+1 and r+2 only and leaves on r+3: two
     turns, 3 of the 4 quadrants. The doc ("3 turns face the other three quadrants") holds only for the first kind.
     In a local game (yl54 vs yl50, DefaultLarge seed 370, scratch dl54), 455 of team 1's 540 pickups (84%) came
     after a move from out of reach. So most looks were one turn short of the designed sweep.
     Fix (sw85): Baby.lookLeft, set to SENTRY_LOOK by take() (both branches), spent by each turn the look makes
     (sweep() now returns whether it turned); look() also stops more than SENTRY_LOOK rounds after the pickup round.
     A pickup ahead of the move behaves as in sw83; one after the move looks on r+1, r+2 and r+3.
   - sweep() makes no turn when the turn is not ready, but sentry() and look() still return true, so the rat stands
     a round. A baby's turn cooldown is 10 per turn (cooling 10 a round) plus 10 or 20 after a thrown rat's landing
     (InternalRobot.hitGround / hitTarget); the look and the sentry need nothing carried and no enemy in view, so
     this is rare. In sw83 such a round also uses up a round of the look's window; in sw85 it spends no look turn.
   - sentry(): no slip found. The king test reads every listed king (G.cheb <= 2, a king's 3x3 sees the mine
     square); a stalled post walk (Nav.stall > 6 on the mine as Nav.target) parks that mine for 100 rounds and the
     rat wanders there as in yl54; the post walk targets the mine tile itself and stops at Chebyshev SENTRY_R.
2. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none. sweep() always turns clockwise, so mirrored twins on reflection maps sweep in
   opposite senses; each covers all four quadrants in four turns, so coverage is the same.
3. *Latent special values:*
   - SENTRY_LOOK 3 is exactly the three unseen quadrants; 4 or more would repeat the first one.
   - The look acts on raw cheese DELIVER_IDLE (10) to DELIVER_AT - 1 (29). A rat at a mine with such a load takes
     the delivery branch first, so it looks and then delivers; it never holds the sentry post with a part load. That
     is yl54's order (DELIVER_IDLE), unchanged.
   - late (after round 1960) and the enemy half skip the look.
4. *Dead or misleading code:*
   - The Cfg.SENTRY_LOOK and LookTest docs claim the full sweep for every pickup (see 1); sw85 corrects both.
   - **Telemetry:** Debug.WHY (the bytecode tag) has no slot for 'Y' (sentry) or 'L' (look). Like 'F' and 'Q'
     before them (audit 2026-11-23-yk1), they are written as 31 (unknown), so in replica replays sentry and look
     turns decode as '?' (tools/smuggle.py). The champion is not changed for it; giving them slots needs the
     bot's alphabet and tools/smuggle.py's to change together (see the test note below).
