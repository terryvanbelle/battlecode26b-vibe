# Bug audit of yl32 (new champion, owner 11-23 new-champion clause, incremental since 11-23 19:48)

yl32 was crowned at 00:05 PDT on 11-24: +36 vs yl25 on 271 games (149-122), against the > +23 it needed. It was
submitted to the replica as #476 (accepted). bots/ratbot = yl32 (package ratbot; every file matches bots/yl32).

**What yl32 is:** yl25 + STANDOFF_EQ, one new switch and one helper. Every other file is byte-identical to yl25.

| switch | yl25 (previous champion) | yl32 | where it acts |
|---|---|---|---|
| STANDOFF_EQ (new) | absent: the standoff holds when the enemy's health >= ours | true: it holds only when the enemy's health > ours | Baby.war's standoff branch, through Baby.standoffFoe |

- *Why it was tried:* the engine lets a rat grab a watching enemy only when the target's health + HEALTH_GRAB_THRESHOLD
  (0) is below the grabber's (RobotControllerImpl.assertCanCarryRat). So neither of two equal rats can grab the other
  from the front, and holding off an equal enemy gave away the first bite for nothing. yl31 (no standoff at all) was
  the wide arm, and is -14 vs yl25 on 358 games at the crowning.

**Review scope (the diff only):**
- Baby.java 1446-1451: the standoff condition now calls `standoffFoe(nearestEnemy.health, G.rc.getHealth())` in place
  of `nearestEnemy.health >= G.rc.getHealth()`, plus a one-line comment.
- Baby.java 1470-1474: `standoffFoe(eh, h)` = `Cfg.STANDOFF_EQ ? eh > h : eh >= h`.
- Cfg.java 196-202: the STANDOFF_EQ switch and its comment.
- `grep STANDOFF_EQ bots/ratbot`: read only in standoffFoe; standoffFoe is called only from war.

**Bugs found: none active.**
- *Logic:* the comparison is right for the engine rule. An equal enemy is neither prey (Baby.prey needs less health
  than ours) nor a standoff foe, so the fighter falls through to `Nav.goTo(nearestEnemy.location)`, as yl31 does for
  every enemy. A healthier watcher is still held off, and the hunting and atKing exemptions are unchanged.
- *Health readings:* `nearestEnemy.health` is the turn-start sensing and `G.rc.getHealth()` is live. Nothing between
  them changes our health this turn (actBeforeMove can only bite or trap), so the comparison is current.
- *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none possible. The change compares two health values and has no position or direction
  in it.
- *Latent special values:* none (a boolean switch and plain health integers).
- *Dead or misleading code (comment nits, no behaviour; left for a cleanup bundle, not a newcomer, since a
  comment-only bot would play byte-identical games):*
  1. The Cfg comment says "the one that steps in bites on arrival and wins the even bite race by one bite". That
     holds only when the step ends adjacent, that is, from Chebyshev 2. From dist2 9-20 the walk takes 2-3 steps, and
     an enemy walking in too (any yl32-lineage bot) can arrive first and take the first bite.
  2. The inherited war() comment above it still says "an enemy that sees us (and is not weaker)". The added line
     right under it gives the new rule, so the block reads correctly as a whole, but the older phrase is now
     inaccurate.
