**Bugs found: none.**
1. *Logic, CAT_YIELD:*
   - `catYield()` is `CAT_YIELD_R2 > 0 && !G.coop && nearestEnemy != null && dist2(me, nearestEnemy) <= CAT_YIELD_R2`.
   - nearestEnemy is reset every turn (Baby line 87), and only findEnemies sets it, at war. It is the nearest enemy
     baby that is not behind a wall past dist2 2, or the prey, which prey() takes within dist2 20.
   - So at war, catYield equals the turn's `engaged` flag (line 97), the same test that drops the TrapRisk mask.
     Enemy kings and cats are never nearestEnemy.
   - The yield skips only urgentMove's cat-at-king branch. A yielding gatherer near the cat then takes the flee branch
     (c) or another errand; a fighter takes the war branches.
   - The bite step (actBeforeMove) is untouched. A yielding rat next to the cat still bites it when no enemy rat is in
     reach: the yield acts on movement only.
   - In cooperation catYield is false, so CAT_DEFEND works as before.
2. *Logic, the standoff:*
   - `nearestEnemy.health >= G.rc.getHealth()` is byte-identical to yl25's line (checked by grep against bots/yl25).
   - Engine: a watching rat can be grabbed only by a strictly healthier one (HEALTH_GRAB_THRESHOLD 0). So against an
     equal watcher neither rat can grab the other; sw71 holds off and faces it, where sw72 stepped in to bite first.
   - This is the choice the tournament made: sw71 +34 over sw72, and yl46 (sw72 with this standoff) +36 over sw72
     on 74 games.
3. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none. No constant depends on our side, and both new tests are dist2 bounds.
4. *Latent special values:* none. CAT_YIELD_R2 0 means off (`> 0`), and nearestEnemy is null-checked.
5. *Interaction (not a bug):* from END_CAT (r1400), endCat's own enemy test is nearEnemy(8).
   - So a rat that yields to an enemy at dist2 9-20 can still be walked to the cat by endCat, when the lone king's
     bank is under endCatBar.
   - endCat is its own switch with a tighter enemy bound, so late in the game the yield narrows to dist2 8 for those
     rats.
6. *Dead or misleading code:* none in the code. One comment nit, no newcomer (a comment-only bot plays identical games):
   - The Cfg doc says "an enemy baby within dist2 CAT_YIELD_R2". Precisely, it is the turn's nearest unwalled enemy baby
     or its prey; a walled enemy within dist2 20 does not count (now tested).
