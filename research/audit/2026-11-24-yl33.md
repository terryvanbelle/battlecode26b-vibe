# Bug audit of yl33 (new champion, owner 11-23 new-champion clause, incremental since 11-23 19:48)

yl33 was crowned at 02:03 PDT on 11-24 (rank after pulling tr516-tr522): +30 vs yl32 on 315 games (175-140), against
the > +24 it needed. It was submitted to the replica as #477 (accepted). bots/ratbot = yl33 (package ratbot; every
file matches bots/yl33).

**What yl33 is:** yl25 + PREY_WEAKEST. It was built on yl25 before yl32's crowning, so its diff from the previous
champion yl32 (yl25 + STANDOFF_EQ) has two parts:

| switch | yl32 (previous champion) | yl33 | where it acts |
|---|---|---|---|
| PREY_WEAKEST (new) | absent: Baby.prey is the nearest weaker enemy baby within dist2 20 | true: the weakest such baby (lowest health; the nearest of equals) | Baby.prey, called once, from Baby.findEnemies |
| STANDOFF_EQ (gone) | true: the standoff holds only against a healthier watcher | absent: yl25's rule, it holds against a watcher with at least our health | Baby.war's standoff branch |

- *Why it was tried:* it imitates AdamTan12_Icarus (replica 39382, 11-23). In 35-45% of our rat duels our rat began
  30+ health lower; we lost about 95% of those, and Icarus struck first in about 60% of them.
- *STANDOFF_EQ:* its removal restores yl25's line exactly (`nearestEnemy.health >= G.rc.getHealth()`), which is
  inherited code and was not re-read. yl38 (yl32 + PREY_WEAKEST, entered at 01:57) is therefore yl33 + STANDOFF_EQ,
  and now tests whether the two switches stack on the champion.

**Review scope (the diff only):**
- Baby.java 1601-1611: prey's doc comment, and the key `k = PREY_WEAKEST ? health * 64 + d : d`, compared where d was.
- Cfg.java 196-200: the switch and its comment.
- What reads the pick: prey's one caller, findEnemies (Baby.java 155-162), and tryCarry, which makes the grab itself.
- `grep PREY_WEAKEST bots/ratbot`: read only in prey.

**Bugs found: one latent logic slip (fix newcomer yl39); no crash, mirror slip or special value.**
1. *Gatherers lose near prey (logic slip).*
   - findEnemies lets a gatherer hunt prey only within dist2 8 (`role != GATHER || dist2 <= 8`), but prey now ranks every
     weaker rat within dist2 20 by health.
   - So when the weakest weaker rat is at dist2 9-20 and another weaker rat is at dist2 3-8, prey returns the far one,
     the gate rejects it, and the gatherer hunts neither. yl32's nearest-first prey hunted the near one.
   - Adjacent rats (dist2 <= 2) are not affected: tryCarry grabs any grabbable adjacent enemy, whatever prey picked.
     Fighters are not affected either: they have no gate.
   - Fix (yl39): prey takes a reach, and a gatherer's is 8 (Cfg.FIX_GATHER_PREY, a temporary FIX_ flag, owner #36).
2. *The key:* health * 64 + d orders by health, then distance, because d <= 20 < 64. The largest key (100 * 64 + 20)
   cannot overflow, and the strict `<` keeps the first robot of equal keys, as before.
3. *Walls:* a wall hides prey as before. walled() runs only for a candidate that would beat the current pick, so the
   WALL_BLIND_CAP budget (6 traces a turn) is spent much as under the nearest rule.
4. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none possible (health and dist2 only, no position or direction).
5. *Dead or misleading code:* none in the diff. The findEnemies comment ("the nearest enemy baby not behind a wall, or a
   prey we can grab") stays accurate. Dropping STANDOFF_EQ makes war()'s inherited "(and is not weaker)" phrase accurate
   again (nit 2 of the yl32 audit).
