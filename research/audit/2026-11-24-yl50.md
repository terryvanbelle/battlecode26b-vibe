**Bugs found: none.**
1. *Logic:*
   - `spawnFloor(f, low, siege)` is `low ? LOW_FLOOR : max(f, KING_RESERVE)`, then `min(r, siege)` when siege > 0.
     Econ.floor multiplies it by our live kings.
   - King.spawn applies it to the core boot (CORE_FLOOR), the war spawn (WAR_FLOOR) and the hard floor every spawn
     must clear (SPAWN_FLOOR, also capped at 2 x the rounds left). So under LOW_BABIES (8) live babies, one king spawns
     down to a bank of 20, not 50.
   - `low` reads the baby count off the rat cost: 4 x floor(n / 4), less the extra kings. It is under 8 exactly when
     fewer than 8 babies live (one king), so the switch acts in the 4-7 band.
   - At a 20 bank a king with no income pays its 2-a-round upkeep for 10 rounds (50 gave 25). After that, an empty
     bank costs the king 10 health a round. That is the bet the tournament took: bodies before the reserve when few are left.
2. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none. No spatial code is involved.
3. *Latent special values:* LOW_FLOOR now equals LAST_FLOOR (20). Not a bug:
   - At 0-3 babies (rat cost 10) King.spawn sets the siege argument to LAST_FLOOR, and `low` always holds there, so
     spawnFloor returns min(LOW_FLOOR, LAST_FLOOR) = 20 either way.
   - So LAST_FLOOR is inert in yl50. Raising it above 20 would do nothing (the min keeps 20); lowering it would still act.
   - Behaviour is unchanged, so there is no fix newcomer.
4. *Dead or misleading code:* none. Cfg line 51's comment gives the value's history (yl44's dose, sw73's stack,
   sw71's 50 from usqualsbot) and is accurate.
