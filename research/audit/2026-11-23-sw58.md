# Bug audit of sw58 (new champion, owner 11-23 new-champion clause)

sw58 was crowned at 13:06 PDT: +26 vs sw54 on 230 games, just over the +26 it needed (sw54 had 876). It was
submitted to the replica as #470 (accepted). This is the third audit under the owner's 11-23 rule (TRAINING_ALGORITHM §2.7), after yk1 and sw54.

**What sw58 is:** sw54 + sw56's kill finishing, one line in Cfg.java:

| switch | sw54 | sw58 | where it acts |
|---|---|---|---|
| FINISH_K | 5 | 8 | Baby.finishCheese: the most extra damage a baby buys to turn a bite into a kill |
| FINISH_FLOOR | 200 | 100 | Baby.finishCheese: the least bank from which a baby buys it |

- *Origin:* sw56 put this change on sw53 (+22 over sw53 at 282 games). sw58 ported it to sw54.
- *Size:* two values. There is no code change: the code is sw54's, audited this morning in
  [2026-11-23-sw54.md](2026-11-23-sw54.md).

**Review scope:**
- *Baby.finishCheese* and its three callers: Baby's rat bite (the best target in reach), and GrabMicro's bite after a
  failed grab and its plain bite.
- *The engine's bite* (InternalRobot.bite, RobotControllerImpl.attack):
  - the damage is 10 + ceil(√cheese);
  - the cheese comes from the biting rat's own carried cheese first, then from the team bank;
  - canAttack(loc, cheese) needs the rat's cheese plus the bank to cover it.
- *What the floor competes with:* the spawn floors (KING_RESERVE 300 per king), the trap gates (reserve + 20 for a rat
  trap from our half) and the king's own cheese bites (King.biteCheese: KING_BITE_MAX 100 above KING_BITE_FLOOR 300).

**Method:**
- Every use was read in its caller's context, and the cost against the engine's formula.
- *Play:* measured on the tournament runs against the 10 opponents both bots played (145 sw58 games), with sw54's games
  weighted by sw58's opponent mix.

## Active bugs (the code pays more than the engine needs)

1. **The finishing bite overpays: k² where (k − 1)² + 1 is enough.**
   - *What the docs say:* Cfg: "A baby pays up to FINISH_K × FINISH_K"; finishCheese: "k*k for +k".
   - *What the engine does:* the damage is 10 + ceil(√c), so +k needs only c = (k − 1)² + 1. For k = 8 that is 50, not 64.
   - *Cost:* 2(k − 1) cheese a kill: 0, 2, 4 … 14 for k = 1 … 8. sw54's FINISH_K 5 overpaid at most 8; sw58's 8 overpays up to 14.
   - *Carried over:* the yk1 audit found it (CheeseTest's header says so), and yl5 fixed it inside the 11-fix bundle. The
     bundle rated level in runs/sp005, but the fix was never measured alone.
   - *The king does the same:* King.biteCheese pays k² within KING_BITE_MAX 100. The least-cost rule lets it afford
     more kills within the same budget: +10 for 82, where k² stops at +10 for 100.
   - *Fix newcomer:* yl21 (FINISH_LEAST): babies and the king pay (k − 1)² + 1. The kill is the same, and the cheese saved
     goes to spawns and traps.

## Latent special values

| switch | sw58 | special values |
|---|---|---|
| FINISH_K | 8 | 0 = off (k ≥ 1 never passes). Every k up to the value is paid in full, so the most a bite costs is FINISH_K² (64; 50 with the fix). Above 9 it reaches a full baby's health range: FINISH_K 90 would pay 8100 for one bite. |
| FINISH_FLOOR | 100 | 0 = finish from any bank. **The floor is a gate, not a reserve:** a bite starts at a bank of 100 and may spend 64 of it, leaving 36. Each later bite in the same round reads the lowered bank, so the bank cannot fall below FINISH_FLOOR − FINISH_K² (36) through finishing. |

- **The floor reads the bank alone.** The engine takes the biting rat's carried cheese first. A gatherer carrying 64
  cheese with a bank of 80 does not finish, though its own load would pay. This is small, and left as it is.

## Misleading or dead code (no behaviour change)

1. **King.biteCheese's doc says "the least that kills now"**, but the code pays k², not (k − 1)² + 1 (bug 1).
2. **CheeseTest's message "the king pays the least that kills"** pins biteCheese(18, 1000) == 64, but 50 kills too. The
   check is right about the code, and the message is wrong about the engine. With the least-cost fix the expectation
   becomes 50.
3. **Cfg's "A baby pays up to FINISH_K * FINISH_K above FINISH_FLOOR"** reads as a reserve. FINISH_FLOOR is a gate: the
   bite may take the bank below it (latent values, below).

The fix newcomer corrects all three: the King and Cfg docs in the bot, and the CheeseTest message, which now reads the
switch by name.

## Tests (11-23, at the crowning)

- **Compliance.**
  - There is no test/sw58 folder: sw58 changed values only.
  - `bash tools/unit-tests.sh` (ratbot = sw58) passes 2023 bot checks: BankTest 10, BotTest 1404, CheeseTest 7,
    FixTest 512, FwdTrapTest 4, MechTest 55, RaidTest 31. No test was changed for sw58: CheeseTest has read FINISH_K

- **Coverage.** CheeseTest grows from 4 to 7 checks:

  - *every payment of the king kills,* over the target healths it pays for (11-20 within KING_BITE_MAX 100);
  - *the least that kills* only on bots with FINISH_LEAST, and k² on bots without it. The test reads the switch by
    name, as BankTest does, so one file serves both.
- **Changed on purpose, for yl21 (FINISH_LEAST):** the expectations of the payment checks.
  - A baby pays 5 for +3 and (k − 1)² + 1 for +FINISH_K, where it paid 9 and k².
  - The king pays 50 for +8, where it paid 64.
  - The over-budget check reads the price: +8 is refused one cheese below KING_BITE_FLOOR + 50, and paid at it.
    Without the switch the threshold stays at + 64.
  - Before this, yl21 failed one check: the king affords +8 at a bank of 350, which the old check called over budget.
- **Results:** sw58 2023/2023, yl21 2023/2023, sw54 2023/2023, sw60 2023/2023.
- **Not covered by a test:**
  - the bite paths themselves (which target, the turn to face it). BotTest drives Baby's bite, but no test sets a
    finishing target's health and checks the cheese passed to attack();
  - the floor reading the bank alone (latent values, above);
  - the effect of the lower floor on spawns and traps in the same round: a finishing bite can take the bank below the
    spawn floor, which King.spawn reads on the king's next turn.
