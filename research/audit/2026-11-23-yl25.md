# Bug audit of yl25 (new champion, owner 11-23 new-champion clause)

yl25 was crowned at 18:08 PDT: +39 vs yl19 on 256 games (146-110), against the > +24 it needed. It was submitted to the
replica as #475 (accepted). This is the fifth audit under the owner's 11-23 rule, after yk1, sw54, sw58 and yl19.

**What yl25 is:** yl19 + RETREAT_HP 0 alone, one value in Cfg.java (every other file is byte-identical to yl19).

| switch | yl19 (previous champion) | yl25 | where it acts |
|---|---|---|---|
| RETREAT_HP | 40 | 0 | Baby.retreat: a baby at or below it with an enemy within dist2 20 walks to our nearest king, unless already within Chebyshev 2 |

- *Why it was tried (the newcomer's census):*
  - A wounded baby retreated to within 2 of our king.
  - Baby.ammoAway (AMMO_HP 50, AMMO_R 3, AMMO_D2 8) then stepped it back out, away from the king, whenever a healthier
    enemy came within dist2 8.
  - So wounded babies hovered about 3 tiles from our king, where enemies grab them from the front and throw them into
    it.
  - In 6 local games vs yl19, enemy throw damage into our king fell from 348 to 234 a game, and yl25 won 4-2.
- *Code:* yl19's, audited at its crowning in [2026-11-23-yl19.md](2026-11-23-yl19.md). Only the value differs.

**Review scope:**
- *Baby.retreat* (Baby.java 1630) and its one caller, urgentMove's `retreat()` branch (1165).
- *What a wounded baby near an enemy does now:* it falls through to formKing, cashMove, flank, faceEnemy, attack and
  war, in that order.
- *The neighbour rule it worked against:* Baby.ammoAway and Baby.ammo (1615-1627).
- *The replay channel:* Debug.WHY's 'r' and tools/smuggle.py.

**Method:**
- Every use of RETREAT_HP was read in context (`grep RETREAT_HP bots/ratbot`: Baby.java 1631 and Cfg.java only).
- *Play:* the tournament's own games on the shared opponents (10 of them, all 205 of yl25's games outside the yl19
  pairing). yl19's games are weighted by yl25's opponent mix.

## Active bugs

None found in the change. `G.rc.getHealth() > 0` holds for every living rat, so retreat() returns at its first test, as
the Cfg doc ("0 = never") says.

## Mirror-symmetry check

OK.

## Latent special values

| switch | yl25 | special values |
|---|---|---|
| RETREAT_HP | 0 | 0 (or any negative value) = never. A baby has at most 100 health, so 100 or more sends every baby that sees an enemy within dist2 20 back to our king: the army would stop fighting. The retreat's own reach (enemy within dist2 20, stop within Chebyshev 2 of the king) is two literals in Baby.retreat, not Cfg values. |
| AMMO_HP, AMMO_R, AMMO_D2 | 50, 3, 8 | Unchanged. AMMO_HP 0 = off. The step is away from our king, not from the enemy (Nav.flee(king)): the point is to stop being ammunition next to the king, not to escape the enemy. |

## Misleading or dead code (no behaviour change)

1. **Baby.retreat's body is dead at RETREAT_HP 0.**
   - Only its first test runs; urgentMove's `why = 'r'` branch never fires.
   - Debug.WHY and tools/smuggle.py keep 'r', so replays still decode.
   - It costs about 10 bytecodes a war turn per baby (one getHealth and a compare).
   - Owner #36 says dead branches go once a change is established. But removing them shifts the bytecode use, which
     re-rolls fragile games, so the removal would have to enter as a newcomer. Left for a later cleanup bundle.
2. **Baby.ammoAway's Cfg doc** ("it moves away") does not say away from what. It means away from our king, the threat
   being the throw. No change made; noted here.

- **Head to head:** yl25 vs yl19 is inside the 256 games; the BT fit puts yl25 39 points above yl19.
- **Overruns are older than yl25.**
  - The king overruns its 20,000 bytecodes (maxBcKing 20,000-20,007) in about 1% of games, and so do babies (maxBcRat
    17,500-17,509). Maps: DefaultLarge, Meow, Nofreecheese, sittingducks, dirtfulcat, ZeroDay, DefaultMedium,
    popthecork, trapped.
  - yl19 shows it in 9 of 1,156 games, yl25 in 7 of 256 (two of those are the same deterministic cell, tr446 and tr450
    Nofreecheese seed 4).
  - The king's code is the same in both bots, so the king's overruns are not yl25's. Its higher baby share (3 games in
    256 against 3 in 1,156) is a small count: watch it, no fix yet.
  - **Where the king crosses.** Replayed locally, both of yl25's king overruns in tr450 come back exactly (the replica
    and the arena are deterministic):

    | cell | king max bytecodes | round | stage (Debug OVS) |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | yl25 vs yl23, Nofreecheese s4 | 20,005 | r437, 6 rounds before our king died | 4748: inside King.move (the dodges, fighting at NEAR 4) |
    | yl24 vs yl25, sittingducks s1 | 20,007 | r1476 (and r1851) | 4899: after King.move, in publish / Econ.endTurn / KPath.tick |

    - KPath's own work is guarded: every row, mine and pop checks KP_RESERVE (2,500).
    - So the crossing is the cost of move() itself, which on these turns leaves too little for the unguarded end of
      the turn. The same code overruns yl19 in 9 of its 1,156 games.
    - A guard that skips the costliest dodge scoring under a bytecode floor would be a fix newcomer. No newcomer yet:
      about 1% of games, one turn each, and in the Nofreecheese game the king was already dying.
    - sw64 adds work to this same path. It runs only with KING_TRAP_LEFT (4,500) bytecodes left, and its 6 check games
      vs yl25 had no overrun.

## Tests (11-23, at the crowning)

- **Compliance.**
  - There is no test/yl25 folder: yl25 changed one value only.
  - testRetreat was changed when yl25 entered (commit 8ac8360a) to read Cfg.RETREAT_HP. At 0 it checks that a rat at 1
    health next to an enemy does not retreat and stays put. That is the behaviour yl25 changed on purpose.
  - `bash tools/unit-tests.sh` (ratbot = yl25): **2,031/2,031 bot checks pass** (BankTest 10, BotTest 1,412,
    CheeseTest 7, FixTest 512, FwdTrapTest 4, MechTest 55, RaidTest 31). Every tool test passes too (test_tools 111,
    battle 74, mapcensus 313, replaydump 146, reports 71, scoring 113).
- **Coverage.**
  - RETREAT_HP: testRetreat covers both branches, choosing by the value.
  - New at this audit: testAmmo (8 checks), the first test of the rule yl25's story rests on. It covers Baby.ammo's five
    bounds (AMMO_HP, a strictly healthier enemy, AMMO_D2, AMMO_R) and the step away from our king, which a healthy baby
    does not take.
- **Not covered by a test:**

    as a chain;
  - the multi-turn hover that yl19 showed and yl25 removed;
  - the overrun stages (bytecodes are not modelled by the mock).
