# Bug audit of yl19 (new champion, owner 11-23 new-champion clause)

yl19 was crowned at 14:03 PDT: +43 vs sw58 on 208 games, against the > +30 it needed. It was submitted to the replica as
#474 (accepted). This is the fourth audit under the owner's 11-23 rule (TRAINING_ALGORITHM §2.7), after yk1, sw54 and
sw58.

**What yl19 is:** sw54 + FINISH_FLOOR 100 alone, one value in Cfg.java. It is the half of sw58 that paid:

| switch | sw54 (base) | sw58 (previous champion) | yl19 | where it acts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FINISH_K | 5 | 8 | 5 | Baby.finishCheese: the most extra damage a baby buys to turn a bite into a kill |
| FINISH_FLOOR | 200 | 100 | 100 | Baby.finishCheese: the least bank from which a baby buys it |

- *The split:* yl18 (sw54 + FINISH_K 8 alone, sw58's other half) rated −68 vs yl19 on 208 games and was retired at
  14:06. So the floor paid and the larger K cost.
- *Code:* sw54's, audited this morning in [2026-11-23-sw54.md](2026-11-23-sw54.md). The finishing code was audited at
  the sw58 crowning, in [2026-11-23-sw58.md](2026-11-23-sw58.md). Only the values differ.

**Review scope:**
- *Baby.finishCheese* and its three callers:
  - Baby's rat bite (the best target in reach, Baby.java 854);
  - GrabMicro's bite after a failed grab (358) and its plain bite (365).
- *The engine's bite:*
  - the damage is 10 + ceil(√cheese);
  - the cheese comes from the biting rat's carried cheese first, then the bank;
  - canAttack(loc, cheese) needs both together to cover it.
- *What the floor competes with:* the spawn floor (KING_RESERVE 300 per king), the home-half rat-trap gate (reserve
  + 20) and the king's cheese bites (King.biteCheese: up to KING_BITE_MAX 100 above KING_BITE_FLOOR 300).

**Method:**
- Every caller was read in context, and each payment checked against the engine's formula.
- *Play:* measured on the tournament runs against the opponents both bots played. The other bot's games are weighted by
  yl19's opponent mix.

## Active bugs (the code pays more than the engine needs)

1. **The finishing bite overpays: k² where (k − 1)² + 1 is enough.** This is carried over from sw54 and sw58 and is
   unchanged in yl19.
   - *Cost at FINISH_K 5:* 2(k − 1) cheese a kill, up to 8 (25 paid where 17 kills).
   - *The king does the same:* King.biteCheese pays k² within KING_BITE_MAX 100, overpaying up to 18 at +10.
   - *Fix newcomer:* yl22 (FINISH_LEAST, the same patch as sw58's fix newcomer yl21, on yl19).

## Mirror-symmetry check

- **No geometric code changed.** The value feeds a per-rat cheese test (the target's health and the team bank). These
  are the same for both seats.

OK.

## Latent special values

| switch | yl19 | special values |
|---|---|---|
| FINISH_FLOOR | 100 | 0 = finish from any bank. **A gate, not a reserve:** a bite starts at a bank of 100 and may spend up to 25 of it (17 with FINISH_LEAST), so finishing alone cannot take the bank below 75 (83). |
| FINISH_K | 5 | 0 = off. The most a bite costs is FINISH_K² (25). Values above 9 reach far into a baby's health range: FINISH_K 90 would pay 8100 for one bite. |

- **The band below the spawn floor.** Between a bank of 100 and KING_RESERVE 300, spawning and home-half rat traps stop.
  Finishing bites still spend there. This is the trade the tournament rated: yl19 kills 6 more enemy rats a game than
  sw54 (below). Its king starves a little more often (16.7% against 13.0% on 150 games, about 0.9 SE).
- **The floor reads the bank alone.** The engine takes the biting rat's carried cheese first. A gatherer carrying 25
  cheese with a bank of 80 does not finish, though its own load would pay. This is small, and left as it is (as in the
  sw58 audit).

## Misleading or dead code (no behaviour change)

1. **King.biteCheese's doc says "the least that kills now"**, but the code pays k², not (k − 1)² + 1 (bug 1).
2. **Cfg's "A baby pays up to FINISH_K * FINISH_K above FINISH_FLOOR"** reads as a reserve. FINISH_FLOOR is a gate (see
   latent values).

The fix newcomer yl22 corrects both docs. The third doc slip from the sw58 audit, CheeseTest's message, was fixed in
test/ratbot at 13:22 and now reads FINISH_LEAST by name.
