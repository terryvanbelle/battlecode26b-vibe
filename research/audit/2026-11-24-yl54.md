**Bugs found: none.**
1. *Logic:*
   - `babies = (cost - 10) / 10 * 4 - max(0, numKings - 1)`. The rat cost rises 10 for every 4 babies, so this is
     4 x floor(n / 4), less one per extra king. With one king, `babies < 12` holds exactly for n <= 11, so the switch
     adds the 8-11 band (rat cost 30). With two kings the estimate is one lower (n = 12 gives 11: still low).
   - In the band every floor is LOW_FLOOR (20) per king instead of max(f, KING_RESERVE) = 300. That covers the core
     boot (CORE_FLOOR 300), the war spawn (WAR_FLOOR 300) and the hard floor (SPAWN_FLOOR 300, capped at 2 x the
     rounds left). So with 8-11 babies one king spawns down to a bank of 20, not 300: 280 more cheese per king goes
     into bodies.
   - The band is under both caps (peace MAX_BABIES 16, war 20), so the cap never stops these spawns.
   - At a 20 bank a king with no income pays its upkeep for 10 rounds (yl50's audit). With 8-11 babies there is
     usually income, so the risk is a sudden wipe of the army. That is the bet the tournament took.
2. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none. No spatial code is involved.
3. *Latent special values:*
   - LOW_BABIES 12 now equals CORE_SMALL 12, the core count on maps of at most CORE_SMALL_AREA (441) tiles. There the
     boot test (babies < 12) and the low test are the same, so every core boot on a small map reads LOW_FLOOR and
     CORE_FLOOR 300 is inert on small maps. It still acts on larger maps (core 20) for 12-19 babies. Not a bug: the
     test's "LOW_BABIES <= the smallest core count" holds with equality.
   - Raising LOW_BABIES past 12 (the newcomer yl55, 16) makes the small-map band 12-15 low but not core. There only
     the war spawn and the hard floor read it. yl55's test checks that case on a 20x20 map.
   - LOW_FLOOR = LAST_FLOOR = 20 is inherited (yl50's audit).
