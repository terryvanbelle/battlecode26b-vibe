**Bugs found: none.**
1. *Logic, checked against the engine source.*
   - **Who can grab us:** the grab rule is RobotControllerImpl.assertCanCarryRat. A grab needs an adjacent target in the
     grabber's sight and free hands. If the target senses the grabber, it also needs
     `target health + HEALTH_GRAB_THRESHOLD (0) < grabber health`.
     - Every rat in `robots` is one we sense, so only a strictly healthier one can grab us.
     - hunterOf's `r.health <= hp` skip matches that exactly, and its `carryingRobot != null` skip matches the free
       hands.
   - **When a trap fires:** RobotControllerImpl.processTrapsAtLocation fires a trap only when an enemy moves into a tile
     of its 3x3. A rat already standing there when the trap is placed does not set it off.
     - An adjacent watcher grabs without a step, so no trap can catch it. Excluding dist2 <= 2 is right.
   - **Range:** dist2 3-8 is exactly the Chebyshev-2 ring (dist2 4, 5, 8), so every hunter is one step from a grab.
     - BABY_TRAP_DIST2 13 is at least 8, so ratTrapAt's range check never rejects a hunter.
   - **Where the trap goes:** trapTile(adj) takes a front tile within the hunter's 3x3.
     - For a hunter ahead of us (offsets (2,0), (2,1), (2,2) along our facing), every one-step approach to us lands within
       Chebyshev 1 of the chosen tile.
     - For a hunter at our side, only some approach tiles are covered.
     - For a hunter behind us, there is no tile, and tryRatTrap falls through to the gated nearestEnemy path.
   - **Gates:**
     - Action readiness, TRAP_CAP and the RAT_TRAP_MIN_CHEESE floor apply to both paths.
     - The reserve and austerity gate applies only to the nearestEnemy path, as the Cfg doc says.
     - With no hunter, tryRatTrap returns what sw69's did. The `nearestEnemy == null` test only moved below the floor
       test, and both return false.
   - **No stale hunter:**
     - The turn sets `hunter = null`, and findEnemies assigns hunterOf's result unconditionally.
     - actBeforeMove runs after the sensing in the same turn, so no stale hunter reaches tryRatTrap.
     - In cooperation findEnemies does not run, so there is no hunter.
   - **Position:** hunterOf and tryRatTrap both run before the move, from G.me = rc.getLocation().
2. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none in effect. No constant depends on our side.
   - Of two hunters at the same dist2, hunterOf keeps the first in sensing order (`d < hd`). The engine's sensing order
     need not be mirror-symmetric, so mirrored positions could trap different hunters of an equal pair.
   - trapTile's own ties use pickBit (inherited). Not a bug.
3. *Latent special values:* none harmful.
   - `hd = Integer.MAX_VALUE`.
   - Past WALL_BLIND_CAP, walled() returns false: an enemy counts as in the open. So in a crowded turn a hunter behind a
     wall can be chosen and a trap spent toward it.
   - hunterOf runs after prey(), so it never takes prey()'s share of the cap. The cap itself is inherited.
4. *Spending:*
   - The hunter's trap skips KING_RESERVE and austerity, but the RAT_TRAP_MIN_CHEESE floor (100, Econ.floor per king)
     stays.
   - With LOW_FLOOR 50 (spawns spend to 50 per king under 8 babies), the two spend-downs stack, and the bank can go under
     the reserve through traps and spawns both.
   - That is both switches' design (sw72 is their stack), and the tournament measured the stack at +39 over sw69. Not a
     bug.
5. *Dead or misleading code:* none in the code. Comment nits only; no newcomer, since a comment-only bot would play
   byte-identical games.
   - The Cfg PREY_TRAP doc and hunterOf's doc say "dist2 3-8", which is in effect dist2 4, 5 and 8.
   - The yl32 audit's STANDOFF_EQ nits and the sw69 audit's LOW_FLOOR nit stand (inherited code).
