**Review scope (the diff only):**
- Cfg.java 50-51: LOW_BABIES 8 (unchanged) and LOW_FLOOR 50, with the new inline comment.
- The lines that read LOW_FLOOR: `grep LOW_FLOOR bots/ratbot` gives King.java 309 only.
  - King.spawnFloor(f, low, siege) (306-311): `low ? LOW_FLOOR : max(f, KING_RESERVE)`, capped at siege when siege > 0.
  - Its three callers in King.spawn (340-366): the core boot (356, CORE_FLOOR), the war spawn (358, WAR_FLOOR) and the
    hard floor (363, SPAWN_FLOOR, at most the upkeep to round 2000).
  - `low` is `babies < LOW_BABIES`, where babies is the rat-cost estimate `(cost - 10) / 10 * 4 - (kings - 1)`.

**Bugs found: none.**
1. *Logic:* the value only moves the floor of the low band.
   - With 0-3 babies (rat cost 10), `sf = LAST_FLOOR` (20) caps every floor, as before.
   - So LOW_FLOOR 50 sets the floor for rat cost 20 (4-7 babies, one king). It also applies at rat cost 30 with two or
     more kings, since the estimate subtracts one per extra king. LowFloorTest checks both.
   - Econ.floor multiplies it per live king, as the other floors do.
2. *Which path applies it:* low implies babies < 8 <= every core count:
   - CORE_SMALL 12, CORE_BABIES 20 and AUSTERE_CORE 99 (no cut) are all at least 8.
   - So whenever the bank covers cost + 50 per king, the core boot builds. The war spawn's identical test adds nothing.
   - The hard floor is reached only through the boot (no surplus test passes with few babies).
   - This is not a bug, but it is a latent dependency: a core count below LOW_BABIES would leave the low band to the war
     and hard floors. A new LowFloorTest check now pins it.
3. *Late game:* the hard floor's `min(..., 2 * (2000 - round))` can go below 50 from round 1976. That code is inherited
   and unchanged.
4. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* none possible. The change involves only a bank value and a baby count, with no
   position or direction.
5. *Latent special values:* none.
   - 50 per king is 25 rounds of upkeep, against 100 at 200.
   - The floor never drops under the spawn cost: every test compares the bank with cost + floor.
6. *Dead or misleading code (comment nits only; no newcomer, since a comment-only bot would play byte-identical games):*
   - Cfg.java 50, inherited: "With fewer than LOW_BABIES babies every floor drops to LOW_FLOOR". That is true only
     from 4 babies. With 0-3 (rat cost 10), LAST_FLOOR 20 holds instead.
   - The yl32 audit's two STANDOFF_EQ comment nits stand. Its inherited war() comment ("an enemy that sees us (and is
     not weaker)") is inaccurate again on sw69, which carries STANDOFF_EQ.

**Coverage:**
- LOW_FLOOR: LowFloorTest had 9 checks. They cover the switch value, the core boot at 4 babies (builds at 20 + 50, not
  at 69), 7 babies at bank 150, the full floor at 8 babies, two kings (per-king floor, estimate 3) and LAST_FLOOR at
  0-3 babies. Three checks are new this audit:
  - at war with an enemy rat in the king's view, 4 babies build at 20 + 50 and not at 69 (the war spawn's floor);
  - the boot path invariant LOW_BABIES <= min(CORE_SMALL, CORE_BABIES, AUSTERE_CORE).
- STANDOFF_EQ: StandoffEqTest (9 checks), back in test/ratbot.
- Prey: PreyNearestTest is new (11 checks), test/ratbot/PreyNearestTest.java.
  - It checks that Cfg has no PREY_WEAKEST.
  - The nearest weaker rat beats a weaker far one. At equal distance the first sensed is picked.
  - A rat with our health or more is never prey. dist2 32 is out of range. Only rats below our own health count.
  - The nearest weaker rat behind a wall is passed over for the next nearest in the open (plus a setup check).
  - findEnemies: a gatherer hunts the near weaker rat, but not a lone one at dist2 13 (it stays the nearest enemy only).
    A fighter does hunt that one. This closes the yl33 audit's gap ("findEnemies itself" was untested on the champion).
