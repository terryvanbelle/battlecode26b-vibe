## Bugs found: none

1. *Logic:*
   - The early bail calls bailBite only when `hostile()`, the test the turn's own pre-move bite uses (`hostile =
     hostile()`, then `biteEnemies(robots)` in actBeforeMove). So an attacker (role ATTACK) in cooperation bites the
     enemy in reach here exactly as it would without the bail, and a gatherer in cooperation before BREAK_ROUND (30)
     never bites (a bite would be a backstab).
   - The micro's bail calls bailBite only when `!saving`. Inside that block `saving` is true only when GrabMicro.act
     acted (a rescue that keeps the action skips the whole block), and then the turn's pre-move bite is skipped too;
     a micro that scored without acting returns false, and bailBite bites from where we stand, as sw83's biteEnemies
     would have had the bytecodes lasted.
   - bailBite ranks as biteEnemies does, without the turn: king tiles by health first (a king's health is capped at
     600 by the engine, UnitType.RAT_KING and InternalRobot.addHealth, below every biteScore, which is at least
     1001), then Baby.biteScore. `rc.canAttack(t)` is the reach and cone test, so nothing outside the cone is bitten
     (biteEnemies turns to a target behind us; bailBite has no turn by design, it is out of bytecodes). A king whose
     nearest tile is out of reach fails canAttack and is skipped. senseNearbyRobots(8) covers every king centre with
     a tile next to us (offset (2,2) is dist2 8). The finishing bite (finishCheese) is paid as in biteEnemies.
2. *Cost (the one real risk of code that runs at the bytecode floor):* bailBite starts with at least BAIL_BITE_LEFT
   1,500 left (up to BC_BAIL 3,500 on the early bail, GM_BAIL 6,500 on the micro's). Engine costs
   (instrumenter MethodCosts.txt): senseNearbyRobots 100, canAttack 10, attack 10; about 40-50 bytecodes an enemy
   baby, about 270 an enemy king (kingTile's 9 tiles). One to four enemies cost about 300; a full crowd within dist2
   8 (24 tiles) could reach about 1,300, which leaves the turn's end (TAG_MIN_LEFT 400 skips the tag) close to the
   limit. Tournament record (all runs to 03:51): sw91 had a rat turn at the limit (17,500) in 3 of 245 games (1.2%;
   tr749 dirtfulcat 9 A vs sw86, tr746 DefaultLarge 2 A vs sw81, tr743 evileye 4 B vs sw89; one overrun each), sw83
   in 10 of 1,765 (0.6%; 1.4 of 245 expected, P(>= 3) about 0.17). King turns at the limit: sw91 4 of 245, sw83 17 of
   1,765 (bailBite runs only in babies).
   *The three rat-limit games replayed locally* (BC_KEEP; each reproduced the box's winner, rounds, overrun and peak
   exactly). Every one is the same turn shape: the grab micro ran 12,269-13,034 bytecodes from mark 4, the turn bailed
   (lb +1), and the overrun came after the turn's last mark (OVS=-1: RobotPlayer's turn end, the indicator built with
   IND_LEFT 1,000 left and the smuggle tag's padding; inherited). bailBite did not spend there: DefaultLarge s2 r50
   (robot 13548) had its action not ready (cooldown 10 at the turn's start: bailBite returns at its first test);
   dirtfulcat s9 r85 (robot 11072) and evileye s4 r314 (robot 13145) had the micro act (action cooldown +24 within the
   turn, no bite by that rat: `saving` true, bailBite not called). The same three games with sw83 in sw91's seat had no
   overrun, but they diverge from sw91's at its first bail bite elsewhere (rounds 196/745/599 against 181/375/838), so
   they are no same-turn comparison. Verdict: no overrun from bailBite; the micro followed by the turn's end tags is
   inherited from sw83 (10 rat-limit games in 1,765). A possible later newcomer (inherited code, not an audit fix):
   skip the indicator after a micro bail, or raise IND_LEFT; worth at most one lost turn in about 1% of games.
3. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* ties keep the first sensed robot (`s < bs`), as biteEnemies does; the sensing order
   is the engine's, so twins on a symmetric map may break a tie between two equally ranked enemies differently.
   Inherited behaviour, not new in kind; no change.
4. *Latent special values:* BAIL_BITE_LEFT must stay below BC_BAIL (3,500) for the early bail to bite at all;
   BailBiteTest asserts BAIL_BITE_LEFT < early < BC_BAIL < mid < GM_BAIL.
5. *Dead or misleading code:* `if (r.team != G.them) continue;` in bailBite repeats the filter of
   `rc.senseNearbyRobots(8, G.them)`: dead and harmless (a few bytecodes a robot). Not worth a newcomer.
