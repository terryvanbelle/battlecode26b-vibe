## Bugs found: none

1. *Logic:*
   - The branch is reachable: formation()'s cap test returns early only at `Comms.numKings >= min(formCap, KING_SLOTS)`
     without rich(); formCap is max(FORM_MAX 3, FORM_MAX_WAR 0) = 3, so at 2 kings the cut branch runs.
     CutKingTest asserts CUT_KING_MAX <= min(formCap(false), KING_SLOTS) and that the war gate (FORM_WAR_FROM 300)
     is open by CUT_KING_FROM.
   - With CUT_KING_MIN 2 and CUT_KING_MAX 3 the rule fires only at exactly 2 kings: it is the generic third-king rule
     (FORM3_BANK 1200 banked, FORM_BABIES 16) without the income test and without the FORM_RETRY gap, inside
     r1000-1150. A lone king never gets a second king from it (sw95's double-formation starvation case).
   - **Note (by design, not a bug):** the spacing is `round - formStart > FORM_TTL` only. A cut site that does not fill
     is closed after FORM_TTL (60) rounds and, on the lead king's next turn, the predicate holds again, so a failing
     site is reopened back to back: at most 3 sites (r1000, ~r1062, ~r1124) before FORM_LAST_ROUND, each pulling babies
     within FORM_PULL_R2 for 60 rounds. The generic rule waits FORM_TTL + FORM_RETRY (210). Inherited from yl42/sw95.
     A FORM_RETRY gap would be a newcomer, not an audit fix (the tournament is shut down).
   - Site choice: pickFormSite first, then ringSite on the guessed mirror, the same order as the generic rich()
     branch; `formSite == null` returns without opening, as elsewhere.
2. *Cost:* cutKing is a handful of int comparisons in the lead king's turn; the site search is the inherited
   pickFormSite/ringSite and runs only on the turn a site opens. Tournament results tr770-784 (scratch tally of
   results.jsonl): rat turns at the limit (17,500) in 3 of sw96's 451 games (0.7%) against sw91's 4 of 676 (0.6%) in
   the same runs; no exceptions in either.
3. *Mirror and symmetry slips:* the predicate reads no position; the site functions are the inherited ones, so twin
   kings on a symmetric map decide alike. None.
4. *Latent special values:* CUT_KING_FROM 0 switches the rule off. CUT_KING_MIN 0 or 1 brings back sw95's behaviour
   (a second and third king from one king and the bar banked, which starved all three kings in sw95's smoke).
   CUT_KING_BANK equals FORM3_BANK (CutKingTest asserts it); a lower bar than FORM3_BANK would make the rule cheaper
   than the generic third king.
5. *Dead or misleading code:* Cfg.java has two javadoc blocks before the one CUT_KING line (sw95's, then sw96's); only
   the second attaches, and the first alone ("with fewer than CUT_KING_MAX kings") no longer describes the rule.
   Cosmetic; merge them in a later refactor.

**How often it fired:** in tr770-784, sw96 formed two kings beyond its first (a third king) in 12 of 451 games (2.7%),
sw91 in 2 of 676 (0.3%), sw95 in 49 of 442 (11%); a second king about equally (sw96 154 of 451, sw91 231 of 676: 34%
each). About 11 extra third-king games (12 against the 1.3 sw91's rate predicts) cannot carry all of a +27 lead (about 4%
of the score, ~18 of 451 games) even if each turned a loss into a win, so part of the margin is likely noise; memory
retire-after-crowning (the winner's curse) applies if the tournament restarts. In its
4 smoke games sw96 formed the second king only.
