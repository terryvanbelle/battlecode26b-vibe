# Bug audit of yk1 (owner 11-23 07:18 PDT)

The owner asked for a bug audit of the local champion yk1, with every bug fixed in a variant entered in the local
tournament, and the unit tests brought up to date.

**Variants entered at 08:13 PDT:**
- **yl5** = yk1 with every fix below.
- **yl6** = yl5 with WAR_GATHER 1. yk1 played WAR_GATHER 1 (see bug 2), so yl6 measures the other fixes alone.

**Review scope:** all of Baby, King, Nav, BitFlow, Flow, KPath, Sym, TrapRisk, Comms, G, GrabMicro and Debug.

**Method:**
1. Four reviewers read the code: roles and combat, king and economy, navigation and flow, shared code and
   telemetry.
2. Every switch was cross-checked against the zero-guards removed by the 11-18 dead-code cleanup (60e001e5).
3. Every claim was verified against the code, and engine claims against bc26-engine-src.

Each fix has a test that fails on yk1. Run them with `bash tools/unit-tests.sh yl5`.

## Active bugs (they changed yk1's play)

1. **FIGHT_POST was nearly inert.**
   - *The bug:* a fighter at war was meant to hold a contested home-half mine. The filter ran after the sort, and
     only while the candidate count equalled the home-half count, a test written when gatherers took home mines
     only. The king lists each mine's twin, so every known mine is a candidate. The filter therefore held only
     until the first enemy-half mine was known.
   - *Measured:* fighters took enemy-half mines in 46% of their war turns, as gatherers do.
   - *Fix:* filter the home-half candidates first, then sort. If no mine is contested, take any home-half mine.
     If no home-half mine is known, take any mine.
   - *Note:* yk1 took the title with FIGHT_POST 10 (from 12). That change mostly reached fighters that knew no
     enemy-half mine.
2. **WAR_GATHER 0 played as 1.**
   - *The bug:* the 11-18 cleanup dropped `Cfg.WAR_GATHER > 0` from the war-gatherer branch while the value was 2.
     The big swing sw08 (11-22, "forward hold") set 0, and every champion since has kept it. In all of them, war
     gatherers still ignored non-adjacent enemies.
   - *Consequence:* sw08's forward hold (FIGHT_POST 8 + WAR_GATHER 0) was tested with both of its switches broken.
   - *Fix:* yl5 plays 0 as documented (everyone fights). yl6 keeps 1.
3. **Late scouts never sought.**
   - *The bug:* only fanFrontier set Flow's fan frame. A scout heard in the king's gatherer order after the scouting
     window (rrSlot 8, 12, ...) never ran it. Its frame stayed empty, which fanOk rejects everywhere, so its
     exploreHome found no target.
   - *Fix:* exploreHome sets the frame itself.
4. **Failed rushers scouted.**
   - *The bug:* every rat drew `scout` from a slot hash at birth. Attackers turned gatherer by RUSH_LIFE kept it,
     so a quarter of them ran the scout logic.
   - *Fix:* `scout` starts false. Only gatherers named in the king's order scout.
5. **Dead formed kings stayed in the baby estimate.**
   - *Engine fact:* a rat that becomes a king stays in numBabyRats for the rest of the game. becomeRatKing never
     decrements it, and a king's death decrements only numRatKings. The rat cost is
     10 + 10 × floor(numBabyRats / 4).
   - *The bug:* yk1 subtracted the live kings but one, so a formed king that died stayed in the estimate as a
     phantom baby. The estimate fed the spawn caps and the formation test.
   - *Fix:* each formed king adds one to a shared counter, Comms.FORMED (index 20, unused before), on its first
     turn. The estimate, King.liveBabies, subtracts that counter.
6. **Kill bites overpaid.**
   - *Engine fact:* a bite spending c cheese deals 10 + ceil(sqrt(c)), so +k damage needs (k−1)² + 1.
   - *The bug:* Baby.finishCheese and King.biteCheese paid k². For example, the king paid 64 for an 18-hp rat that
     50 kills, and refused it outright on a 50–63 budget.
7. **TrapRisk never ran on 60x60 maps.**
   - *Engine fact:* the engine charges an allocation exactly its length in bytecodes.
   - *The bug:* the gate asked for 3 per tile plus BC_OPTIONAL: 18,800 on 60x60, against a baby turn of 17,500.
     So rats on DefaultLarge, Nofreecheese, cheeseguardians and wallsofparadis never had trap-risk marks. On 50x50
     maps the marks waited for a turn with 15,500 bytecodes left.
   - *Fix:* the gate asks for one per tile.
8. **Scouts stood at dirt they could not dig.**
   - *The bug:* pipeFrontier waited while digging was not allowed (too little cheese), however long that lasted.
   - *Fix:* after FLOW_HOLD turns the tile is marked HELD for PIPE_HELD rounds and the walk is dropped, as for a
     robot in the way.
9. **The rescue-king bar left out the promotion.**
   - *The bug:* CASH_END and the endgame cat bar require both kings' upkeep to the end, but not the 50 cheese the
     promotion itself costs. A rescue formed exactly at the bar could starve both kings.
   - *Fix:* `Baby.cashBar` adds RAT_KING_UPGRADE_CHEESE_COST.

## Mirror-symmetry slips

These broke the rule that twin rats make mirrored choices. Each one gave the two sides different play on the
same map.

10. **Side frame on a centre line.**
    - *The bug:* on thunderdome both kings sit on the middle row. Both sides took fv = +1, so the twins sampled the
      same tiles.
    - *Fix:* a zero offset follows the other axis's sign once the symmetry is known to be rotation, and is +1 under
      a reflection that keeps the line. The frame is set again when Sym.mask changes.
11. **Cat distances from the corner tile.**
    - *The bug:* a cat is 2x2. catAtKing, the cat-trap defence, the king's cat dodge and carryCat measured from its
      low corner, and the twin cat's low corner is not the mirror of that corner. On 30x24 the yk1 version of
      catAtKing disagrees with its mirror in 114 positions.
    - *Fix:* all four measure from the body (Baby.catDist4, Baby.chebToBody). The spawn ring's cat term does too.
12. **Integer map centres.**
    - *The bug:* the spawn goal used (W/2, H/2), explore() ranked against ((W−1)/2, (H−1)/2), and the coop endgame
      cat target used (cx2/2, cy2/2) for both sides. On an even side all three are off-centre.
    - *Fix:* all use doubled coordinates, or the home side's centre tile. The spawn ring's corner penalty is scaled
      to match (−50 → −200).
13. **Throw-dodge ties.** Equal steps went to the first in G.DIRS, often toward the carrier, and not to the twin's
    mirrored step. *Fix:* fewest flights, then the farthest from the carriers (summed dist²), then G.hand.
14. **BitFlow ties.** A tie between equal turns took each candidate's own hand, which reflections reverse, then
    G.DIRS order, which rotations do not keep. It was wrong for 25% of ties even on rotation maps. *Fix:* the side
    of the target that G.hand picks first, as Nav.choose does.
15. **Nav.momentum.** The hand of h.rotateLeft() decided instead of the hand of h.

## Latent bugs (a documented special value did the wrong thing; yk1's values never reach them)

The 11-18 cleanup removed zero-guards whose switches were all non-zero at the time. sw35 (RUSH_LIFE 0) hit one of
them and never rushed.

**Restored with tests** (what each special value now does, and what yk1 did with it):

| switch | now | yk1 |
|---|---|---|
| RUSH_LIFE 0 | no rusher gives up | every rusher gave up on its second turn |
| RUSH_UNTIL 0 | rush spawns never stop | no rush spawns |
| BREAK_ROUND 0 | never | from round 0 |
| END_CAT, END_CAT_COOP 0 | off | from round 0 |
| SCOUT_EVERY 0 | no scouts | division by zero |
| STALL_CAT 0 | off | the errand at once |
| ATK_STUCK 0 | off | home at once |
| WALL_BLIND_CAP 0 | no cap | walled() never ran |
| CARRY_CAP 0 | no cap | took nothing |
| SEEK_IDLE 0 | never idle | idle at once |
| AUSTERE_FROM, FORM_RICH_BANK, END_KING 0 | off | from round 0 / any bank |
| KING_ROAM 0 | off | the anchor tile only |
| BF_GIVEUP 0 | never give up | gave up every turn |
| CAT_FEED 0 | no cat targets; a cat on a flight scores −50 | cat targets kept, scored 0 |
| SWAP_HOLD > 0 | the hold swap skips the loaded-ally test | tested it anyway |
| KP_FRONT_LOW −1 | off | toggled anyway |
| DIG_FIRST false | off | dug first anyway |
| SCOUT_FAN 0 | off | fanned anyway |
| WANDER_R 0 | 3 in both places | 0 in one of them |

The THROW_DODGE doc now says "0 or less = off". The code never had a "compute but never act" mode.

**Removed or corrected with no change in play:**
- **Dead code:**
  - two attacker-only king-bite branches, unreachable because hostile() is true for every attacker;
  - Sym.EXP, a bit that was read and preserved but never set.
- **Telemetry:** Debug.WHY had no slot for 'F' (cat-feed walk) or 'Q' (cash move), so both were logged as 31
  (unknown). They now take the slots of 'H' and 'h', which no bot has set since the s-line. tools/smuggle.py
  decodes both alphabets the same way for earlier bots.
- **Docs:**
  - G.hand and GrabMicro.key now state the known reflection asymmetry (below);
  - DIG_COST now states its safe range, 2 to 6. Flow's 8 cost buckets alias the open-tile bucket at 1 and wrap
    past the popped bucket from 7. The value is 4.

## Checked and kept

- **LAST_FLOOR at the base rat cost.** A formed king's rat stays counted, so after f formations the last stand
  fires at 3 − f or fewer live babies. That is the conservative reading of "3 or fewer". Testing the lower-bound
  estimate instead would fire with up to 6 live babies after one formation. Only its override of a lower
  SIEGE_FLOOR is fixed.
- **Reflection-class tie-breaks** from a candidate's own hand (pickBit, GrabMicro.key, the fan's sign and other
  pickBit sites). Rotation keeps them mirrored. Reflected twins rank such ties in reverse.
  - This is deliberate: chir stays 1 since the side-symmetry bundles s9k, u2s and u5y failed on the replica, and
    owner rule #21 allows breaking symmetry for an advantage.
- **KPath's side frame on a centre line.** It has the same zero-offset case as bug 10: both thunderdome kings
  take fv = −1. The frame only orders ties between equally short king steps. Left as is: no effect on play was
  found.

## Tests (11-23)

- **Runner:** `tools/unit-tests.sh [BOT]` tests a candidate. It compiles bots/BOT as package ratbot with
  test/ratbot/*.java, and test/BOT/*.java replaces same-named files. Every class runs, and the script fails at the
  end if any class failed. When BOT is crowned, move test/BOT/*.java into test/ratbot/.
- **MechTest** (new, 55 checks on yk1):
  - roles, rush life, throw dodge, baby caps, spawn floors, reserve, austerity, trap gates, carry threat;
  - feed score, attacker stuck, idle, fighters' home post.
- **CheeseTest:**
  - test/ratbot (4 checks) holds yk1's bite costs, moved out of BotTest;
  - test/yl5 (7 checks) holds the least that kills, including an engine-consistency loop.
- **AuditTest** (test/yl5, test/yl6; 56 checks):
  - *latent switches:* RUSH_LIFE, RUSH_UNTIL, BREAK_ROUND, END_CAT(_COOP), SCOUT_EVERY, STALL_CAT, ATK_STUCK,
    WALL_BLIND_CAP, CARRY_CAP, SEEK_IDLE, AUSTERE_FROM, FORM_RICH_BANK, END_KING, WANDER_R and CAT_FEED;
  - *active fixes:* FIGHT_POST, WAR_GATHER, liveBabies and the FORMED count, cashBar, cat-distance mirror
    invariance, centreTile, the thunderdome frame, throw-dodge ties, TrapRisk on 60x60 and the late scout.
- **Not covered by a test** (each a one-line guard or tie rule, checked by reading):
  - the scout flag at birth, the undiggable-dirt hold, the BitFlow and momentum ties, the spawn-ring and
    explore() centres;
  - the guards on KING_ROAM, BF_GIVEUP, SWAP_HOLD, KP_FRONT_LOW, DIG_FIRST and SCOUT_FAN. These are static finals
    the compiler inlines, so a test cannot set them.
- **Checked against yk1:** a probe on yk1 confirms the new checks fail there. catAtKing has 114 mirror mismatches,
  the throw dodge picks EAST, TrapRisk is unallocated on 60x60, and a war gatherer does not engage at WAR_GATHER 0.
- **Other test updates:**
  - FixTest K7 sets FORMED;
  - BotTest's fake controller lays rat traps;
  - tools/test_tools.py accepts either telemetry alphabet.
- **Results:**
  - `bash tools/unit-tests.sh` (ratbot = yk1, plus the tool tests) passes: 1404 + 4 + 512 + 55 bot checks and
    every tool test;
  - `bash tools/unit-tests.sh yl5` and `bash tools/unit-tests.sh yl6` pass: 2034 checks each.
