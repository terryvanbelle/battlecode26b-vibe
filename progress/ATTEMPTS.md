# Attempts

Every bot attempt (a snapshot under bots/), oldest first, with its score at each stage of the acceptance funnel. Generated by `tools/attempts.py render` from `progress/attempts.csv`; update a row with `tools/attempts.py set`.

**The funnel**:
1. **Unit tests and diagnostics**: `tools/unit-tests.sh` passes; single diagnostic games (driver) show no overruns, exceptions or stuck rats.
2. **Gauntlet** (VM, `tools/arena.py matrix`): mean points share against the six strongest field bots on DefaultSmall, DefaultMedium and cheesefarm, both sides (36 games, SE about 2 points; *top6 3 maps*), on Excavation, Stash and UncomfortablyClose (*top6 new maps*), and enemy deaths per our death (*k/d*). Older attempts were scored on 4-8 map panels (*top6 (older panels)*) or against examplefuncsplayer (*vs example*, win rate).
3. **Rating**: Bradley-Terry rating over every game in progress/games.csv (`progress/ELO.md`) and its field score (expected score against each field player).
4. **Replica**: the best build is submitted to the galaxy replica and scrimmages the ladder (`progress/ladder.md`, `progress/rating-history.md`).

**Milestone** rows (`progress/milestones.csv`) sit where they fall in time, between the attempts made before and after them.
